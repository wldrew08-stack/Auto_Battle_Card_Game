I was thinking that in order to make the game more engaging we could add "objectives" so the game isn't purely battle focused. 

Objectives:

- Hold down the fort: If a Player controls at least 3 lanes they gain 1 summon point next round.
- Perfect Defense: If a Player takes no health damage they are able to draw 1 extra card next round while respecting the draw limit of 5.
- Breakthrough: If a Player wins a lane with a vCard costing 5 points or less they can draw one extra card the next round. 

Furthermore, I was thinking that in order to make the game more interesting and maybe diverging from the standard deck a little we could add more cards with different abilities. 

New Cards: 

- Scout: This card is able to reveal an opponent's infantry card of your choosing before revealing your own cards to allow for changes within your play. 
- Commander: This card boosts adjacent cards' attacks by +2 to cards that are 5 or lower in number. This card has an attack of 5 points. 
- Regroup: This card allows a Player to remove a played card in a previous round.
- Trap: An opposing infantry card gets its attack lowered by -2 (is able to be placed in front of your own infantry cards).
These cards would be limited to 2 per deck in order to level the gameplay. These cards would also be completely new cards separate from the original suits within the decks.
